Shipping Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Cash on Delivery order requests: You may submit a request for an in-stock product with a confirmed price. The request is not final until MARF contacts you and confirms availability, delivery charges and the order.

Delivery charges and locations

Enter your city and delivery address accurately when submitting a Cash on Delivery request. If the delivery charge is not shown at checkout, MARF will confirm the charge with you before dispatch. Please do not assume delivery is free.

Order confirmation and timing

Submitting the checkout form records a request; it does not guarantee stock or dispatch. MARF will contact you to confirm the product, total payable amount including any delivery charge, delivery availability and the expected delivery estimate before dispatch. Delivery estimates can vary by location and courier.

Delayed or damaged parcels

If your parcel is delayed or arrives damaged, contact MARF through WhatsApp as soon as possible and include your order details and photos where relevant.

Questions

For help with an order request or delivery details, contact us through WhatsApp. A submitted request is not confirmation that an order has been accepted or shipped.
