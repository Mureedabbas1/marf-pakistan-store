Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about MARF Pakistan. In-stock products with confirmed prices can be submitted as Cash on Delivery order requests. MARF will contact you to confirm the order and delivery details.

Can I place an order?

You can submit a Cash on Delivery request for a product that appears in the live catalogue with a confirmed price and available stock. Submitting the form is a request, not final acceptance of the order. MARF will contact you to confirm availability, delivery charges and the order before dispatch.

Which products can I order?

Only products shown in the live catalogue with a confirmed price and available stock can be requested. Planned category cards and editorial images are for browsing and do not indicate that an item is available.

How do I pay?

The website currently accepts Cash on Delivery order requests only. Card payments are not enabled. Your order is not final until MARF confirms it with you.

How much is delivery?

Delivery charges and estimated timelines may depend on your location. If a delivery charge is not displayed at checkout, MARF will confirm it with you before dispatch. See the Shipping Policy.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Please contact MARF before confirming an order to ask about the applicable return, exchange and refund terms for that item. See the Returns & Refunds Policy. Do not send an item back without contacting us first.

How can I contact MARF Pakistan?

Use the Contact page, or reach us through WhatsApp. You can also follow Instagram, Facebook and TikTok.

How do I care for bags and accessories?

Care depends on the material. Follow the product label, avoid harsh cleaners unless approved by the manufacturer and store items in a cool, dry place. Read our wallet care guide for more general tips.

Where can I find helpful articles?

Visit the MARF Journal for practical guides on bags, wallets, beauty essentials and everyday organisation.

Submitting a Cash on Delivery form records an order request only. MARF will confirm product availability, delivery charges and the order with you before dispatch. Please read the Shipping Policy and Returns & Refunds Policy before proceeding.
