# MARF PAKISTAN — Brand Guide

## Brand mark
The approved concept is the intertwined **M + R** monogram, representing MARF and the personal meaning of the R, paired with the MARF PAKISTAN wordmark.

## Core identity
- **Brand name:** MARF PAKISTAN
- **Tagline:** STYLE • QUALITY • YOU
- **Primary background:** Matte black `#090909`
- **Primary accent:** Champagne gold `#D4AF67`
- **Light neutral:** Ivory `#F4F0E8`
- **Display type:** Cormorant Garamond
- **Interface type:** Manrope

## Usage
- Keep the approved M+R emblem and MARF PAKISTAN wordmark together on primary brand placements.
- Use black and champagne gold for premium packaging and digital headers; use ivory for readable body text.
- Keep generous clear space around the logo. Do not stretch, rotate, add extra effects, or change the letter proportions.
- For small favicons, use a simplified monogram rather than the full tagline.
- Use the tagline exactly as: **STYLE • QUALITY • YOU**.

## Important asset note
