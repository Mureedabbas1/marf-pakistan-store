Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MARF Pakistan handles information when you visit this website.

Information you provide

If you contact us through WhatsApp or a social media platform, that service may process the information you send, such as your name, account details, and message. Please do not send payment card details or other sensitive personal information in a message.

Information collected by the website

This static website may receive standard technical information through hosting and security services, such as browser type, approximate request information, and pages requested. We do not currently offer account registration or checkout on this preview website.

You can review Google Ads Settings and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The cookie banner lets you accept or reject optional analytics. Necessary browser storage is used to remember your choice. Advertising is not currently enabled on this preview website; if advertising technologies are enabled later, this policy and consent controls will be reviewed before launch.

Information you send us is used to respond to your enquiry and support the website's development. We do not sell personal information.
