A Practical Guide to Choosing Everyday Gifts

A thoughtful gift is one that fits the person receiving it. You do not need to guess at the latest trend; start with their routine, preferences and the things they use often.

By the MARF Pakistan editorial team · 4 minute read

Notice their routine

Do they commute, study, travel often or prefer to keep things minimal? A work bag may suit someone who carries a laptop, while a compact wallet or organiser may be more useful for someone who travels light.

Keep personal style in mind

Look at colours, shapes and materials they already use. If you are unsure, understated designs and versatile colours may be easier to pair with different outfits. Avoid assuming that everyone prefers the same style.

Choose usefulness over novelty

Think about whether the item solves a small everyday problem or replaces something worn out. Check size, storage, care instructions and practical features. Useful details often matter more than extra decoration.

Set a comfortable budget

Decide your budget first and compare quality, usefulness and care requirements within it. A higher price does not automatically mean a better gift, and you should not feel pressure to spend more than you can comfortably afford.

When you are not certain

Ask a subtle question about what they need, choose an item with a clear use, or check whether exchanges will be possible. For online purchases, confirm delivery timing, return rules and product details before paying.

Before you buy

  • Does it suit the recipient's real routine?
  • Is the size, colour or format appropriate?
  • Are materials and care instructions clear?
  • Does it fit your budget?
  • Are availability and delivery dates confirmed?
