Returns and Refunds

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Important: Cash on Delivery requests may be submitted for eligible in-stock products, but an order is not final until MARF confirms it with you. Please ask about the applicable return, exchange, cancellation and refund terms before confirming your order.

Before confirming your order

Return, exchange, cancellation and refund eligibility can depend on the product and the circumstances. Contact MARF before confirming your order to ask for the applicable terms. Please do not assume that change-of-mind returns, exchanges or refunds are automatically available.

Damaged, faulty or incorrect items

If an item arrives damaged, faulty or different from what you ordered, contact MARF promptly through WhatsApp and provide your order details and clear photos where possible. We will review the issue and explain the next steps. Any rights that apply under Pakistani law are not excluded by this page.

How to contact us

Do not send a product back before contacting MARF and receiving return instructions. This helps us identify the order and confirm the correct return address and process.

Questions

For launch updates, contact us through WhatsApp. A refund is not automatic when an order request is submitted; contact MARF to discuss an order-specific issue.
