    How orders work

    1. Pending — a new order just came in.
    2. Confirmed — you called or messaged the customer.
    3. Processing — the order is being packed.
    4. Shipped — handed to the courier.
    5. Delivered — cash collected.
    6. Cancelled — stock is returned automatically.

    Reopening a cancelled order needs enough stock.
